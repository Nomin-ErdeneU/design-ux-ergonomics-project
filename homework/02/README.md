# Homework 02: User research, persona, and user journey

**FigJam board:** [Homework 02 - User research](https://www.figma.com/board/2NFlEzbSYkC9IR2ieqQkqd/Project---Posture-Ergonomics?node-id=4-141&t=b79hLHgLbPHyqok8-4)

## Summary

I did a short interview (about 15 minutes) with one student (Participant A, self-reported) who works on programming and database coursework in long laptop sessions. I asked about past behavior: how they take breaks, what they do when their back or neck is uncomfortable, and how timers and notifications affect their focus. The notes are anonymized. Because this is a single self-reported account, the persona and insights are hypotheses to be checked with more participants.

## Main findings

- Breaks happen at natural task boundaries (a test passes, code compiling), not at fixed times.
- A fixed-time Pomodoro alarm in the middle of hard problem-solving was dismissed and the tool was deleted.
- Posture problems are noticed late, after stiffness starts, and fixed with improvised solutions.
- A popup message cost about ten minutes of focus, so a posture signal must not break concentration.

## What the board contains

- Research plan
