# Homework 01: Finding an idea and exploring the problem

I created a FigJam board that maps a real problem situation for my semester project, a workstation and posture ergonomics assistant.

**FigJam board:** [Homework 01 - Posture Ergonomics](https://www.figma.com/board/2NFlEzbSYkC9IR2ieqQkqd/Project---Posture-Ergonomics?node-id=4-140&t=b79hLHgLbPHyqok8-4)

## Summary

Students who work at a computer for hours want to finish their task without neck and back pain. They don't notice their posture drifting, and standard break timers interrupt their focus, so they ignore them. Strain builds up and the reminders get dismissed.

## What the board contains

- **Problem:** the specific problem, who it affects, and in what situation it occurs
- **Goals and constraint:** the user goal, the organization goal, and the most important constraint, kept separate
- **Problem sentence:** one sentence using the user, situation, goal, obstacle and consequence framework
- **Success criterion:** in a 60+ minute test session, participants hold correct posture for a larger share of the time with the tool than without it, and rate the task disruption 2 or less on a 5-point scale
- **Assumption map:** each claim with its source (observation, professional rule or assumption) and its risk. The riskiest assumption is that users will accept posture feedback.
- **First pitch:** a short visual pitch of the idea
